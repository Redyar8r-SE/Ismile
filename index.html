About iSmile

iSmile started in 2021, when italk Foundation and the Kurdistan Dental Association brought science, clinical practice, industry and the dental community together in Kurdistan.

    Organized by

    italk Foundation

    Visit italk.krd ↗

    italk was founded in Sulaymaniyah in 2019 by a group of young professionals who saw that the city had few places to share knowledge and grow professionally. Their first meetings took place in coffee shops.

    Today italk brings researchers, speakers and community leaders together through conferences, seminars and workshops, with local and international support.

    Scientific areas

    Eight tracks covering the full range of modern dentistry.

    01

    Implantology and oral surgery

    Implant planning, placement and surgical techniques.

    02

    Prosthodontics and restorative

    Crowns, bridges, veneers and full-mouth rehabilitation.

    03

    Endodontics and periodontology

    Root canal treatment and gum health.

    04

    Orthodontics and pediatric

    Braces, aligners and children’s dental care.

    05

    Aesthetic dentistry

    Smile design, whitening and cosmetic restorations.

    06

    Oral medicine and pathology

    Diagnosis and care of oral diseases and lesions.

    07

    Digital dentistry

    Intraoral scanning, CAD/CAM and 3D printing.

    08

    AI in dentistry

    AI tools for diagnosis and treatment planning.
