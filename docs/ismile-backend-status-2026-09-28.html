The backend: what we have

The registration, payment and ticket system for iSmile 2026, with its staff admin. It is finished and tested, saved on GitHub, and not yet on the server. This document explains what it does, what is in it, how it was tested, where it is, and what is left before it can take real registrations.

1The main rules it follows

  • Only people who paid are registered. A filled-in form waits in a temporary area while the person pays. It becomes a registration only when the payment company (Psoola) confirms the exact amount. If nobody pays, the form and its photo are deleted automatically.
  • Students upload a photo of their student ID with the form and pay straight away. There is no approval step. The photo is stored inside the database and shown in the admin.
  • No refunds, for tickets and workshops. A double payment gives one ticket; Finance handles the extra money.
  • The event has no seat limit. Lunch (per day) and each workshop have limits.
  • Workshops are managed in the admin (add, change seats, hide) and booked by phone by the office.

Every link between tables is enforced by the database, and fixed lists (statuses, languages, payment methods) only accept allowed values. 9 read-only "views" give ready lists (registrations, lunch days, students, workshops with their people, sponsors, exhibition, money per day). Nobody needs to open the database itself: the admin shows it all.
