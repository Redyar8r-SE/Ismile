Scan the QR from the email or the PDF, or type a name. A second scan says “already checked in at 08:55”. No internet? The printed list from the night before.

What is different from a “card form on our site”: in step 5a Sara's card number goes to Psoola, never to ismile.krd. That keeps us out of the card-industry security rules and means there is nothing on our server for anyone to steal. It is the same way airlines, hotels and most shops do it: your site, their payment page, back to your site.

What happens when something goes wrong

✕

Card declined, app payment refused, or she closes the page

Planned

Step 7 never says “paid”, so no ticket. Sara stays unpaid in the admin. Within a few minutes she gets an email with a “Pay now” link — one tap starts a fresh Psoola payment for the same registration. No form to fill again. One reminder after 3 days.

⏱

Bank is slow — Psoola confirms 20 minutes later

Planned

The “Checking your payment…” page tells her “we will email you”. When the confirmation arrives — or when our 10-minute check asks Psoola and hears “paid” — the ticket goes out. She never has to come back to the site.
