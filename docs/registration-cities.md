The City dropdown uses one shared list in data/cities.json for all public and office registration forms, including guest tickets and company/booth registration. Values are stable English city names; displayed names are translated into English, Arabic and Sorani Kurdish. Sulaimani retains the existing website spelling. Existing registrations with other city values keep those values when edited.

The list contains the capitals of all 19 governorates, including Erbil, Sulaimani, Duhok and Halabja. Geographic references checked on November 9, 2026:

- Iraq National Investment Commission: https://investpromo.gov.iq/investment-map/
- Iraq Central Statistical Organization administrative units: https://www.cosit.gov.iq/AAS/AAS2012/section_1/5.htm
- United Nations report confirming Halabja as the nineteenth governorate: https://iraq.un.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/S2025323_EN.pdf
- Presidency of the Kurdistan Region, four governorates: https://presidency.gov.krd/en/kurdistan-region/

Guest-ticket city data stays in the local guest details and resulting registration. It does not change the provider ticket request or payment calculation. Saved requests without a city retain their original fingerprint.
