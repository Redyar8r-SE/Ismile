How to use the iSmile admin

A step-by-step guide for anyone on the team, even if you have never used it. The admin is where you see and manage everything: registrations, payments, lunch lists, students and their ID photos, workshops, sponsors, exhibition booths and the check-in at the door. You never need to open the database itself. The admin shows it to you as simple pages.

The one rule to remember

A person is registered only after paying. Filling in the form is not enough. The moment the payment company (Psoola) confirms the exact amount, the person appears in the admin with a ticket. If they do not pay, they never appear, and their form is deleted automatically.

2Signing in the first time (phone code)

You need: your email, the password the Owner gave you, and a phone with Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator (free in the App Store / Play Store).

  1. Open the admin address. Type your email and password, press Sign in.
  2. Owner and Finance: the Phone code page opens. In the phone app press +, choose Scan a QR code, and scan the square picture.
  3. The app now shows iSmile with a 6-digit number that changes every 30 seconds. Type that number and press Turn on.
  4. Write the key under the picture on paper and give it to the Owner for the sealed envelope in the office. It lets you set up a new phone if yours is lost.

3Signing in every day

  1. Email and password, Sign in.
  2. Open the phone app, type the current 6-digit number, Sign in. A code works only once; if it is refused, wait for the next one.
  3. When you finish, press Sign out (top right), especially on a shared computer.
Signed out by itself?After 30 minutes without using it, or 12 hours in total. Just sign in again. This protects people's data.
5 wrong triesThe account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again.
Lost your phoneTell the Owner. They press Reset phone code on the Users page, and you set it up again.
Forgot passwordThe Owner sets a new one on the Users page.

5Finding a person (Registrations)

Click Registrations in the menu. You see everyone registered, newest first, with a ✓ PAID or ★ FREE badge next to each name.

To find someone

  1. Type in the search box any of: name (even part of it), phone (0750… or +964…), email, the reference ISM26-… or the ticket number T26-….
  2. Press Search. Press Clear to see everyone again.
  3. Click the name or the reference to open the person's page (next chapter).

Filters (the boxes next to the search)

StatusRegistered (paid + free), only Paid, only Free ticket, or Cancelled.
TicketProfessional or Student.
LunchDay 1, Day 2, or no lunch.
City / LanguageWhere they live, and the language of their emails.
Possible duplicatesTwo registrations with the same phone or email. Often a family sharing a phone; check before changing anything.
Email failedPeople whose ticket email could not be delivered (usually a typing mistake in the address).

Export to Excel (this list) downloads exactly what you see, with the filters applied.

Fix a wrong email, phone or name

  1. Press Edit name, email or phone.
  2. Correct the field and press Save.
  3. Wrong email? After saving, press Resend ticket. It goes to the new address.
  4. Name changed? A new ticket is made and emailed automatically. The old QR code stops working (the name is printed on the certificate).

8Booking a workshop by phone

Workshops are booked only by phone, by the office. Only registered people (who paid the event ticket) can take a workshop seat. Click Workshops.

When someone calls to book

  1. In 📞 Book a caller, type their name or phone, press Find. Check the ✓ PAID next to their name: they are registered. Nobody found? They have not paid the event ticket. Register them first (chapter 10).
  2. Choose the workshop. The price fills in by itself; change it only if a different price was agreed.
  3. Choose Paid?: ✗ Not paid yet if they will pay later (e.g. at the door), or ✓ Paid now if you received the money.
  4. If Paid now: type the amount received. It must be exactly the price, or it is refused. Choose how: cash, transfer or Psoola.
  5. Press Book the workshop. The seats left on the website go down by one at once.

Mark a workshop paid later

Press People next to the workshop. People who have not paid are at the top. Type the amount, choose how, press ✓ Mark paid.

No refunds. Once a workshop is paid, staff cannot undo it or remove it. Only the Owner can, to correct a mistake (for example, money recorded on the wrong person). Sign-in sheet downloads the list for the trainer.

9Adding and changing workshops (Owner)

We do not know yet how many workshops there will be, so the Owner adds them here. Everything is saved in the database, and the website's workshop page changes at once. Open Workshops and scroll down to ⚙ Manage the workshops.

Add a new workshop

  1. Press + Add a new workshop.
  2. Write the name in English, Arabic and Kurdish. Trainer and company are optional. Leave the names empty only if the title is not announced yet (the website then shows "Coming soon").
  3. Type the price per seat (IQD; 0 = not set yet), the number of seats (this is the limit), and choose the picture for the website card.
  4. Press Add the workshop. It is in the database, on the website, and ready for booking.
− +One seat fewer / one seat more. The seats are the limit: nobody can be booked past it. They can never go below the people already booked.
EditChange the name, trainer, company, price, seats or picture. A new price counts for new bookings; people already booked keep the price agreed with them.
HideTakes it off the website and stops new bookings. The people already booked stay. Show again puts it back.
DeleteOnly for a workshop nobody was ever booked on (the button appears only then). Otherwise use Hide, so the history stays.
Workshops are managed only here. The "Site content" editor still has the wording of the workshops page (for example the booking phone number), but not the workshops themselves.

10Registering someone who calls

For someone who wants to register by phone. On the Registrations page press + Register a caller (phone).

  1. Type their names, phone, email (the payment link and the ticket go there), city.
  2. Choose the ticket. For a student, write the university (you have seen their ID).
  3. Choose the language of their emails, and the lunch days.
  4. Press Send the "Pay now" link.
They get an email with a Pay now button. They are registered only after paying: then they appear in Registrations with a ticket. If they do not pay within the days set in Settings (3 days), the details are deleted automatically.

Owner only: a free ticket

For a guest, a speaker's assistant, etc. Fill in the same form, write the reason in the Owner box, and press Register with a free ticket. They are registered at once with a ★ FREE ticket, emailed to them. The reason and your name are recorded.

Closing a red row

  1. Call or email the person; agree what happens (iSmile does not refund; for example, the extra payment counts for a workshop).
  2. Write what was agreed in the box on the red row and press Reviewed, close. It leaves the red box on the Dashboard.
Daily totalsMoney confirmed per day. Compare with Psoola's daily report; if a day is different, look for a red row.
Messages from the payment companyEvery "payment changed" message Psoola sent. First place to look when someone says "I paid but got nothing".

12Check-in at the door

For summit days. Works on a phone. Door staff have the Check-in role and see only this page.

  1. Press 📷 Scan QR and point the phone at the QR code on the person's ticket (phone screen or paper). Or type the ticket number T26-… or the name, and press Find.
  2. Check the name on the screen against the person. The card also shows their lunch days and workshops.
  3. Press ✓ Check in. Done: they may enter.
"Already checked in at 09:12"This ticket was already used. Someone may be using a copy. Send them to the registration desk.
Red "not genuine" / "old ticket" / "no valid ticket"A fake QR, an old ticket after a name change, or a cancelled ticket. Do not let them in; send them to the registration desk.
No internetThe evening before, export the Registered list to Excel and print it. Tick names on paper, enter them afterwards.

13Sponsors & booths

Requests from the sponsor form on the website. A person handles each one; nothing is sold automatically. Sponsors and exhibition booths are different, so they have separate tabs at the top: 🏆 Sponsors and 🏪 Exhibition (booths).

  1. Open a request (click the company). Assign it to yourself and add notes about calls.
  2. Move it along: New → Contacted → Agreed → Paid → Confirmed. Or Declined / Waiting list.
  3. A request can be Confirmed only after it is Paid, and a tier cannot take more sponsors than its spots. Only the Owner can override either.

For a clean list to send or print, use Lists → Sponsors or Lists → Exhibition (booths).

16Excel and printing

  • Registrations → Export to Excel: the list you see, with your filters.
  • Lists → Export to Excel / Print: lunch lists for the caterer, students, everyone.
  • Workshops → Sign-in sheet: one workshop's people, with a signature column.
  • Payments → Export to Excel and Sponsors → Export to Excel (Finance / Owner).
Exported files contain people's personal data (names, phones, emails). Do not post them in group chats, delete them when you no longer need them, and never send them outside the team. Every export is recorded with your name.

17Questions people ask

"I paid but I did not get a ticket."
Search their name, phone or email in Registrations. Found with ✓ PAID? Check the email address on their page; fix it if wrong and press Resend ticket (also ask them to check spam). Not found? Their payment was not confirmed, so they are not registered. Look at Payments (a waiting or red row with their name) and Messages from the payment company; if Psoola took the money, Finance contacts Psoola. Never promise a ticket before the admin shows ✓ PAID.
"I filled in the form but I am not in the list."
Correct: the form alone is not a registration. They must pay. They can fill in the form again on the website, or you register them by phone (chapter 10) and they pay with the link.
"I paid twice."
They have one ticket. Finance sees a red Duplicate row on Payments, contacts them (iSmile does not refund) and closes it with a note.
"My name is spelled wrong on the ticket."
Open their page, Edit, correct the name, Save. A new ticket is emailed automatically; the old one stops working.
"Can I pay the event ticket in cash at the office?"
No: event tickets are paid only through Psoola (Visa, MasterCard, FIB, FastPay), so every payment is confirmed. For a guest, the Owner can give a free ticket. Workshops can be paid in cash.
"Is this student really a student?"
Open their page and look at the ID photo they sent. There is no approval step; if something is clearly wrong, tell the Owner.
"I want to cancel and get my money back."
Tickets and workshops are non-refundable. You may cancel the registration (the ticket stops working) but no money is returned.
"Where are the student ID photos?"
Inside the database. See one on the student's page, or all of them in Lists → Student IDs. They are deleted automatically 90 days after the summit.
"A new workshop was announced / a workshop needs more seats."
The Owner adds it, or presses + for more seats, on Workshops → ⚙ Manage the workshops. The website changes at once.
"Can the event be full?"
No. The event has no seat limit: everyone who pays can join. Only lunch and each workshop have limits.
Something looks wrong or broken.
Do not try to fix it by guessing. Take a screenshot and send it to the Owner. Everything is logged, so nothing is lost.

18Golden rules

Always
  • Use your own account; sign out on shared computers.
  • Check the ✓ PAID badge before promising anything.
  • Type workshop money exactly as received.
  • Write a reason when you cancel or give something free.
  • Keep the Dashboard's red box empty.
Never
  • Share your password or phone code.
  • Send exported lists outside the team.
  • Promise a refund. iSmile does not refund.
  • Send someone's personal ticket link to another person.
  • Practise on the real admin: use the test admin.
